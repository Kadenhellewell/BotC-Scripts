# Nomination Mayhem

This script warps the nomination process into a minefield. The Witch and Fearmonger can turn a nomination into a death sentence, the Organ Grinder muddies who's even voting, and the Devil's Advocate or Goblin can flip the consequences of an execution entirely. Even the Virgin and Zealot tie their fates directly to being nominated or executed.

Expect town meetings full of second-guessing, where deciding who to nominate is as dangerous as deciding who to execute.
