# Violence

This script is unapologetically brutal. Direct-kill roles like the Slayer, Assassin, Huntsman, Bounty Hunter, Boomdandy, and Psychopath give both town and evil the power to end a life in an instant, while the Imp, Shabaloth, Po, Zombuul, and Zenomancer bring a rotating cast of vicious demons to the night phase.

Expect a script with a high body count and little patience for a quiet game.
