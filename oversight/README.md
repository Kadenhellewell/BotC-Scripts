# Oversight

This script leans on watching and information. The Steward, Snake Charmer, Al-Sa'ahir, Night Watchman, Philosopher, and Puzzlemaster all give the town ways to keep an eye on each other, while the Devil's Advocate quietly protects whoever is under threat. Looming above it all is the Kazali, a demon that assembles its own council of minions at the start of the game.

With so much scrutiny in play, the real challenge is figuring out who's actually watching, and who's being watched.
